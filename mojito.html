A refeshing mint and lime drink bursting with cool citrus flavors.

⏱️ 5 mins | 🍴 1 Servings | ⭐ 4.8 ( reviews)

Ingredients

  • • 10 fresh mint leaves
  • • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • • 2 tsp sugar
  • • 120 ml soda water
  • • Ice cubes
  • • Lemon slices (for garnish)
  • • Fresh mint sprig (for garnish)

Calories

110 kcal

Difficulty

Easy

Prep Time

5 mins

Cook Time

0 mins

Instruction

1

Muddle the Ingredients

Add the lime wedges, sugar, and mint leaves to a glass. Gently muddle to release the mint aroma and lime juice.

2

Add Ice Cubes

Fill the glass with plenty of fresh ice cubes.

3

Pour the Soda

Top with chilled soda water and stir gently to combine all the flavors.

4

Garnish the Drink

Add a fresh mint sprig and a lemon slice for a refreshing presentation.

5

Mix Gently

Stir gently to keep the drink fizzy while blending the ingredients.

6

Serve Chilled

Serve immediately and enjoy your refreshing Mojito.

𝒞𝒽𝑒𝒻'𝓈 𝒯𝒾𝓅

Gently muddle the mint leaves instead of crushing them to avoid a bitter taste and keep the drink fresh and aromatic.
