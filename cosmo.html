A vibrant pink cocktail with perfect balance of citrus and cranberry flavors.

⏱️ 5 mins | 🍴 1 Servings | ⭐ 4.8 (8 reviews)

Ingredients

  • • 45 ml vodka
  • • 15 ml orange liqueur
  • • 30 ml cranberry juice
  • • 10 ml fresh lime juice
  • • Ice cubes
  • • Orange peel (for garnish)
  • • Lime slice (optional)

Calories

180 kcal

Difficulty

Easy

Prep Time

5 mins

Cook Time

0 mins

Instruction

1

Fill the Shaker

Add ice cubes to a cocktail shaker.

2

Add the Ingredients

Pour in the vodka, orange liqueur, cranberry juice, and fresh lime juice.

3

Shake Well

Shake vigorously for about 15–20 seconds until the drink is well chilled.

4

Strain the Drink

Strain the mixture into a chilled cocktail glass.

5

Garnish

Garnish with an orange peel twist or a fresh lime slice.

6

Serve Chilled

Serve immediately and enjoy your refreshing Cosmopolitan.

𝒞𝒽𝑒𝒻'𝓈 𝒯𝒾𝓅

Chill the cocktail glass in the refrigerator or freezer for a few minutes before serving to keep your Cosmopolitan refreshingly cold.
