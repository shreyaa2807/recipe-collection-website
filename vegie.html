Veg Supreme Pizza

Loaded with fresh vegetables, melted cheese and vibrant flavors in every bite.

⏱️ 30 mins | 🍴 4 Servings | ⭐ 4.8 (7 reviews)

Ingredients

  • • 1 pizza dough
  • • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • • 200g mozzarella cheese
  • • 1/4 cup bell peppers(red,yellow,and green),sliced
  • • 1/4 cup onion,sliced
  • • 1/4 cup mushrooms,sliced
  • • 1/4 cup sweet corn
  • • 1/4 cup black olives,sliced
  • • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • • 1/2 tsp oregeno
  • • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • • Salt to taste
  • • Black pepper to taste

Calories

250 kcal

Difficulty

Easy

Prep Time

15 mins

Cook Time

15 mins

Instruction

1

Prepare the dough

Roll out the pizza dough on a lightly floured surface.

2

Add the sauce

Spread pizza sauce evenly over the dough.

3

Add the cheese

Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese evenly over the sauce.

4

Add the vegetables

Top with bell peppers, onions, mushrooms,sweet corn and black olives.

5

Brake

Drizzle olive oil, sprinkle drid oregano,salt and black pepper. Bake in the preheated oven for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.

6

Garnish and serve

allow the pizza to cool slightly, slice and serve hot.

Serving Suggestion

Serve hot with garlic bread, a fresh garden salad, or crispy potato wedges.

Drink Suggestion

Enjoy with chilled lemonade, iced tea, cola or a refreshing fruit juice.

For a crisper crust and extra flavor, lightly sauté the mushrooms and onions before adding them to the pizza, and finish with a sprinkle of fresh herbs after baking.
