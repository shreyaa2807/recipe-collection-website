Schezwan Noodles

Spicy stir-fried noodles tossed with fresh vegetables anf fiery schezwan sauce.

⏱️ 25 mins | 🍴 2 Servings | ⭐ 4.8 (12 reviews)

Ingredients

  • • 200g Hakka noodles
  • • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • • 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • • 1/2 cup cabbage, shredded
  • • 1/4 cup carrot, julienned
  • • 1/4 cup capsicum, sliced
  • • 2 spring onions, chopped
  • • 2 tbsp Schezwan sauce
  • • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • • 1 tsp vinegar
  • • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • • Salt to taste

Calories

340 kcal

Difficulty

Easy

Prep Time

10 mins

Cook Time

15 mins

Instruction

1

Cook the Noodles

Boil the Hakka noodles until just cooked. Drain, rinse with cold water, and toss with a little oil.

2

Stir-Fry the Vegetables

Heat oil in a wok, sauté the garlic, then stir-fry cabbage, carrots, capsicum, and spring onions over high heat.

3

Add the Sauces

Add Schezwan sauce, soy sauce, vinegar, black pepper, and salt. Mix well until the vegetables are evenly coated.

4

Toss the Noodles

Add the cooked noodles and toss well so they are fully coated with the spicy sauce.

5

Cook on High Heat

Stir-fry for 2–3 minutes over high heat, tossing continuously for a smoky flavor.

6

Garnish and Serve

Garnish with chopped spring onions and serve hot with extra Schezwan sauce if desired.

Drink Suggestion

Pair perfectly with with fresh lime soda, iced tea, or a cool mint mojito.
