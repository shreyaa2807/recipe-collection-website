Hakka Noodles

Classic stir-fried noodles tossed with fresh vehgetables and savory sauces.

⏱️ 25 mins | 🍴 2 Servings | ⭐ 4.8 (15 reviews)

Ingredients

  • • 200g Hakka noodles
  • • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • • 1 tsp garlic, finely chopped
  • • 1/2 cup cabbage, shredded
  • • 1/4 cup carrot, julienned
  • • 1/4 cup capsicum, sliced
  • • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • • 1 tsp vinegar
  • • 1 tsp chili sauce (optional)
  • • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • • Salt to taste

Calories

320 kcal

Difficulty

Easy

Prep Time

10 mins

Cook Time

15 mins

Instruction

1

Cook the Noodles

Boil the Hakka noodles until just cooked. Drain, rinse with cold water, and toss with a little oil to prevent sticking.

2

Stir-Fry the Vegetables

Heat oil in a wok, add chopped garlic, then stir-fry the cabbage, carrots, capsicum, and spring onions over high heat.

3

Add the Noodles

Add the cooked noodles to the vegetables and toss gently to combine everything evenly.

4

Season the Noodles

Add soy sauce, vinegar, chili sauce (optional), black pepper, and salt. Mix well until the noodles are evenly coated.

5

Toss on High Heat

Stir-fry for 2–3 minutes over high heat, tossing continuously for the best flavor and texture.

6

Garnish and Serve

Garnish with freshly chopped spring onions and serve hot.
