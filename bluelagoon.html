A refreshing blue cocktail with citrus flavors and a vibrant tropical twist.

⏱️ 5 mins | 🍴 1 Servings | ⭐ 4.8 (8 reviews)

Ingredients

  • • 30 ml vodka
  • • 30 ml blue curaçao syrup
  • • 120 ml lemonade or Sprite
  • • Ice cubes
  • • Lemon slice (for garnish)
  • • Maraschino cherry (optional)
  • • Fresh mint leaves (optional)

Calories

180 kcal

Difficulty

Easy

Prep Time

5 mins

Cook Time

0 mins

Instruction

1

Fill the Glass

Fill a tall serving glass with plenty of ice cubes.

2

Add the Ingredients

Pour the vodka and blue curaçao syrup over the ice.

3

Top with Lemonade

Add chilled lemonade or Sprite and stir gently to combine.

4

Garnish the Drink

Decorate with a lemon slice, a cherry, and fresh mint leaves.

5

Mix Gently

Give the drink a gentle stir to blend all the flavors.

6

Serve Chilled

Serve immediately and enjoy your refreshing Blue Lagoon.

𝒞𝒽𝑒𝒻'𝓈 𝒯𝒾𝓅

For a vibrant presentation, chill the glass behorehand and use plenty of ice to keep the drink refreshingly cold.
